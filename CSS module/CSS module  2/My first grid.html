My fitness cards

Diet;

A fit and healthy diet should consist of nutrient-dense whole foods balanced across key groups: lean proteins for muscle repair, complex carbohydrates for steady energy, healthy fats for hormone balance, and plenty of fiber from fruits and vegetables to support overall physical performance and well-being.

Exsercise

a warm-up, aerobic activity, strength training, and a cool-down with stretching. Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic work and two strength sessions per week.

Routine

moving often, sleeping well, drinking water, and lowering stress. Small daily habits keep your body healthy and energetic.
